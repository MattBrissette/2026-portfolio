Lab

Re-Aktor

An atmospheric exploration of light, particles, and interactive 3D.

RE-AKTOR is an experimental WebGL experience drawing inspiration from Eastern European industrial architecture, post-Soviet infrastructure, and the mysterious atmosphere of an abandoned cooling silo.

Set inside a fictional, decommissioned cooling silo, the experience combines a procedural particle simulation, atmospheric lighting, generative textures, and reactive sound to create a living, cinematic environment.

I led the visual direction, created the soundtrack, and developed the experience using an AI-assisted creative coding workflow. From shader experimentation and particle behavior to environmental rendering and audio-reactive interactions, the project became an exploration of how design, sound, and technology can come together beyond traditional interface conventions.

mattbrissette.github.io/re-aktor

Loam

A spatial search engine built for discovery, not scrolling. As design engineer, I led the concept, interaction design, and full Three.js/WebGL build, transforming search results into an explorable 3D environment where unexpected connections emerge.

Powered by live data from six sources, users explore clusters, expand searches, pin discoveries, and export findings as Markdown notes.

Built with Three.js, WebGL, GLSL, GSAP, spatial algorithms, and AI-assisted development (Gemini + Claude), supporting thousands of entities in real time.

About

I'm a Visual and Digital Designer with 10 years of experience creating engaging digital experiences across websites, landing pages, e-commerce, and digital products. I combine strong visual design skills with a user-focused approach, translating ideas and business goals into intuitive interfaces and compelling digital experiences.

My work spans visual direction, UI design, wireframing, prototyping, and high-fidelity design in Figma. I collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to bring concepts to life, balancing visual quality, usability, and consistency across different platforms and touchpoints.

I also integrate AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude into my creative workflow to accelerate exploration, prototyping, and execution. With additional experience in 3D, WebGL, motion, and interactive prototyping, I enjoy pushing the boundaries of what's possible in digital design.

My work has been recognized by Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, Applied Arts, WARC, and Behance. Fluent in both English and French.
